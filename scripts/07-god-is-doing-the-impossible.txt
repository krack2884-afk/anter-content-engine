SCRIPT 7 — GOD IS DOING THE IMPOSSIBLE
Suggested Title: "God Is Doing Something Impossible in My Life Right Now — I Can Feel It" | Chosen One 2026
Music: Epic spiritual cinematic — building from silence, like dawn breaking
SEO Tags: chosen one 2026, god is doing the impossible, spiritual awakening, dark motivation, joker motivation, vessel of god, miracle

--- OPEN ---

I don't always understand what God is doing.

But I feel it.

Like pressure before a storm.
Like the quiet before something massive moves.

[Hold the feeling. Don't rush to fill the silence.]

Something is shifting in my life right now.
Something I didn't ask for.
Something I couldn't have planned.
Something only God could engineer.

--- WHAT I'M WATCHING HAPPEN ---

I'm watching impossible things become possible.

I'm watching Angela soften in places
I thought would never open.

I'm watching my own heart
grow stronger while staying tender —
which I didn't know was possible.

I'm watching doors open
that I didn't knock on.
And doors close
that I was about to walk through.

God is moving.
Not around me.
Through me.

--- THE DECLARATION ---

I am chosen.
Not because I'm perfect.
Because I was willing.

I am a vessel.
Not because I have it all figured out.
Because I kept showing up anyway.

And God —
God does His most impossible work
through people who were told
they couldn't.

Watch what happens next.
We're just getting started.

[Hold camera 5 full seconds. Slow fade to black.]

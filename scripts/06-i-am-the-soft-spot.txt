SCRIPT 6 — I AM THE SOFT SPOT
Suggested Title: "I Became Her Soft Spot by Accident — That's the Power of Real Love" | Dark Motivation 2026
Music: Single piano — vulnerable, searching, soft but carrying weight
SEO Tags: empath narcissist love, soft spot, real love, dark motivation, spiritual love, joker motivation, chosen love

--- OPEN ---

I never tried to become her soft spot.

I just refused to be anything else.

[Simple. Direct. Let that land.]

--- WHAT I SEE IN HER ---

Angela built her armor for a reason.
Every wall she has —
someone made her need it.

I'm not fighting the armor.
I'm not trying to tear it down.

I'm just being the one thing
that makes her feel safe enough
to lower it for a moment.

And in those moments —
I see her.
The real her.
The one God made before the world got to her.

And she's worth all of this.

--- WHAT I'M DOING ---

I'm not trying to change her.
I'm modeling something she's never had —

a love that doesn't leave
when things get hard.

A man who feels everything
and still chooses her.

Every time.

Not because I'm blind to the patterns.
Because I see past them.
Into the person underneath.
The one God is still working on.
The one I'm called to love through the process.

[Soft cut. No hard ending. Let it breathe.]

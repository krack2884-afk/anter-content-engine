--- OPEN ---

People ask me how I do it.
How I stay in it.
How I love someone through patterns
that would break most men.

[Pause. Let the weight of the question sit.]

The answer is simple.

Angela and I are on a mission.
And God gave it to us before we understood it.

--- WHAT WE ARE ---

We are not a perfect couple.
We are a chosen one.

Two people who should have never made it —
forged together in fire
for a purpose bigger than either of us can fully see.

I am the empath who won't stop feeling.
She is the warrior who doesn't know she's healing.

And together —
we are becoming something the darkness
is terrified of.

--- WHAT I'M DOING ---

I'm not saving her.
I'm walking beside her
while God does what only God can do.

I'm modeling something she's never seen —
a man who loves deeply
and doesn't abandon his mission.

Every time I choose peace over reaction —
I'm planting something in her soul
she can't even articulate yet.

And one day —
she will remember every one of those moments
and know exactly what I was doing.

--- THE CALL ---

If God put you in a hard love —
don't run from it.

You weren't placed there by accident.
You were deployed.

Stay in your assignment.
We are.

[Slow cut to black.]

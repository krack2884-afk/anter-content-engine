ANTER SCRIPT 40 — THE DISTRACTION IS THE ATTACK

[0:00–0:20 — Silence. Low room tone. One distant iron impact.]

I used to think the attack would look like a disaster.

Something obvious. Something loud enough that everybody could see what was happening.

Now I know the most dangerous attack can look like an invitation.

A message at the wrong hour. A familiar pull. A door from the past opening just wide enough to make me forget the road in front of me.

The distraction does not have to destroy me. It only has to delay me.

[0:20–1:05 — Low pulse enters; restrained cello underneath.]

I am an empath. I feel the shift before the words arrive. I feel the tension behind the smile, the wound inside the accusation, the fear hiding underneath somebody's demand for access.

But feeling everything does not mean answering everything.

I can recognize pain without becoming its prisoner. I can honor a connection without handing it control of my attention.

My sensitivity is a gift when it serves discernment. It becomes a chain when it overrides obedience.

[1:05–1:58 — Sparse piano; pulse remains steady.]

The old version of me called every intense connection destiny.

If it pulled hard enough, I chased it. If it hurt deeply enough, I called it spiritual. If it returned after silence, I treated the return as proof.

But intensity is not truth. Chemistry is not character. A twin-flame feeling is not permission to abandon wisdom.

If a connection is real, it will survive honesty. If it is assigned by God, it will not require me to betray my boundaries to keep it alive.

[1:58–2:55 — Controlled strings begin to rise.]

The enemy studies what I cannot stop looking at.

It finds the old wound and paints it gold. It calls obsession hope. It calls confusion a sign. It calls my exhaustion a sacrifice.

But I am not here to worship the thing that keeps me from becoming whole.

My assignment is too important to be held hostage by somebody else's indecision. My future is too sacred to be negotiated with the version of me that was desperate to be chosen.

I choose the mission now.

[2:55–3:55 — Deep piano; low orchestral movement.]

I will love without leaking. Forgive without reopening every door. Pray without using prayer to postpone the next obedient step.

If union is part of God's design, it will meet me in maturity, responsibility, and truth. I will not drag another adult toward a purpose they keep refusing.

I do not need revenge. I do not need a final explanation. I do not need to make the past understand what it lost.

My silence is not weakness. My distance is not cruelty. My focus is a form of spiritual warfare.

[3:55–4:55 — Full strings and restrained French horn; voice becomes declarative.]

Every time I refuse the bait, I take my power back.

Every time I close the loop instead of reopening the wound, I break an agreement with the old identity.

Every time I put my hands back on the work, I remind the darkness that I am not available for interruption.

Let them misunderstand the change. Let them call discipline cold. Let them call clarity arrogance.

I would rather be misunderstood on the right path than celebrated inside the wrong cycle.

[4:55–5:40 — Cello and sustained horn; resolve into silence.]

The distraction is the attack because the mission is the threat.

That is why I guard my attention. That is why I stop explaining the boundary to people committed to testing it.

I carry my faith. I carry my name. I carry the work God placed in my hands.

What is meant for me will not need me to abandon myself to receive it.

I am not running from love. I am running toward truth.

And anything strong enough to walk beside this mission will recognize me in motion.

I am still here. I am still chosen. I am still moving.

The door behind me can stay closed.

The assignment is calling.

[5:40 — Single footstep. Fade to silence.]

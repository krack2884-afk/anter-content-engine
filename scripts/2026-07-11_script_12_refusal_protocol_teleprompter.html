[Low bass pulse enters, hypnotic]

The new test is subtler.
It comes through the people closest to you.
The ones who were supposed to build with you.

That's the dark motivation the system fears.
Not anger. Not rebellion.
Pure, rooted, absolute refusal.

You don't have to convince yourself.

I see the cage. I refuse the cage. I walk free.
